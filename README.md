Throughout the course of the hackathon, Team Deuterium has worked in a synchronous manner to create a website that monitors fluctuations on the Earth's surface using existing NASA Earth models in order to conceive accurate and reliable readings, Earthify. Earthify utilizes a wide array of different variables that make it adept at fulfilling the challenge provided. The challenge asks for a project that monitors changes in the Earth's surface and classifies them as significant or minor. This is precisely what Team Deuterium has achieved in its Earth data tracking website. Earthify can be a vital tool for aspiring meteorologists, agriculturists, climate enthusiasts, and many more due to its ability to display complicated data in a digestible and understandable manner. Team Deuterium firmly believes that Earthify will have a profound impact on the field of climate science as a whole due to its versatility and ease of use.
